OBENVOICE - ROWAN AND MARA PLAYER VOICES - ATTRIBUTION / MODIFICATIONS

Upstream source material:
  hexgrad/Kokoro-82M voice tensor styles
  https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M
  Upstream repository declaration: Apache License 2.0

Intermediate source material:
  Stalburg Voices Vol. 1 v1.0.1
  20 procedurally modified Kokoro-compatible style tensors generated from
  the pinned upstream tensors recorded in that pack's PROVENANCE.json.

Modifications:
  Rowan and Mara were generated offline by gender-conditioned statistical
  style-space sampling across the corresponding Stalburg style tensors with
  seeded stochastic innovation. They are derivative style tensors, not
  independently trained speakers, recorded human voices or voice clones.
  The exact player-generation program was not supplied with the candidate;
  the recorded method, source/output hashes and technical validation are in
  PROVENANCE.json.
