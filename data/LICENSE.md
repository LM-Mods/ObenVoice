## Source and credit

The current database was generated from a local Obenseuer 0.5.19 character
registry scan supplied for private development. It contains the 292 scanned
game character IDs, exact displayed names and game-provided genders required
for local identity matching. Legacy-only research names and wiki-only records
are excluded.

## Adaptations

ObenVoice restructures limited factual character-identification information
into JSON records used for local voice matching. Scanner diagnostics, scene
observations, research prose, personal paths and other unnecessary source data
are not reproduced.

recipient who shares an adaptation must provide attribution, indicate changes
