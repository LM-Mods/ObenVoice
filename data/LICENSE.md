## Adaptations

ObenVoice extracts and restructures limited factual character-identification
information into JSON records used for local voice matching. The records were
supplemented and corrected using in-game curation, including displayed names,
aliases and gender classifications. Wiki prose, character descriptions and
other unnecessary article text are not reproduced.
